% A: Jan., Feb., March, April, May, June, July, Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov., Dec.

% Q: What should I write for my job title?
% A: Try to be specific, instead of just saying intern or member, write the department, or what your main job was

% Q: My links are too long!
% A: You can shorten your linkedin URL by going to https://www.linkedin.com/public-profile/settings and editing your URL in the top right under "Edit your custom URL"

% Q: Should I ever have a resume longer than 1 page?
% A: No, only exception is if you have a Masters or PhD and need to list your publication, but then that'd be a C.V. and not a resume

% Q: Can I use a font other than Times New Roman?
% A: The more your font stands out the worse it probably looks to a recruiter, don't be concerned about blending in, that's not an issue. It's about making a readable resume and letting your experience do the talking!

% Q: Should I add a summary/brief intro at the top of my resume
% A: You shouldn't, it's generic and most recruiters will skip right over it

% Q: Should I submit a cover letter?
% A: Not unless required, they probably won't read it

% Q: Headshot of myself on my resume?
% A: No, recruiters can unconsciously discriminate

% Q: How long should I keep high school experience/irrelevant experience on my resume?
% A: Replace it as soon as you can, if you can't currently replace it, join a design team and get involved ASAP!

% Q: Should I include where I live in the top line along with my phone number etc.
% A: No, may make recruiter throw your resume if they think you're too far from the place even if you would be willing to relocate
